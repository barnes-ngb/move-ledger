04 / Keep it easy

Our three travel rules

  • Food is decided before we leave. No searching for restaurants mid-drive.
  • Stop intentionally. Wichita lunch, short rests, Buc-ee’s and Razzoo’s pickup. Ardmore is optional.
  • Comfort beats the clock. Stop sooner for Shelly, the kids or the animals; don't push through fatigue or storms.
Pet travel basics

Sunny: secure him in a stable cargo area. Use leashed relief stops away from other dogs and dog parks while his DAPP vaccination is pending.

Bell: use a secured, ventilated carrier. Open it only in a closed room at your parents' house; have litter, water and a quiet space ready.

Never leave either animal unattended in the car.

Before we get on the road

Friday evening: check Saturday's Kansas, Oklahoma and Texas weather and road conditions, confirm China's hours, check coolant and tires following the Forester repair, and text grandparents the ETA.
